# Diary tab: Tonight and Passages (v1 build step 5)

Spec: `docs/spec/v1.md` §4.2.

## What changed

- `Views/Reflection/` is now `Views/Diary/`:
  - `DiaryView` (was `ReflectionView`): modes `tonight` (default) and `passages`. The toolbar switch is now a plain text button ("Passages" / "Tonight"); the old icon-over-caption label was clipped in the iOS 26 toolbar.
  - `TonightView` (was the placeholder `ReflectionTodayView`).
  - `PassagesView` (was `ByQuestionView`): grouped by `passageID`, newest first; serif titles; scrolls to the passage when opened from Tonight.
  - `JournalEntryDetailView` (was `ReflectionDetailView`): unchanged apart from the name and a serif question. Previous/next already moved between entries for the same passage.
- **Tonight**, top to bottom: the date; the stone summary (one row per colour with its roots, nothing on a day with no stones); the passage (serif title, "AN 10.51 · translated by Bhikkhu Sujato", italic serif excerpt with "…" between blocks, "Read the full sutta ↗"); a divider and the question in a larger serif; the editor, the helper text "Each day keeps one entry. Saving again replaces it.", Save and "Saved at …".
- Kept from White Stone: "You've written on this passage N times before." under the question when there are earlier entries; it opens Passages at that passage. Not in the spec; easy to remove.
- If an entry exists for the day, Tonight shows its stored question and its passage, so a later reword or set change doesn't alter what was written against.
- The day follows the clock (03:00 rollover while open, and on returning to the app), but waits while there's unsaved text so a rollover can't wipe it. If saving a new entry fails because 03:00 has passed, a quiet note explains and offers "Go to today's passage"; the text stays in the box.
- Unsaved text is held by `DiaryView`, so it survives switching to Passages and back.
- New `Utilities/StoneDaySummary.swift`.
- DEBUG-only launch arguments for simulator screenshots: `-debug.selectedTab 2` and `-debug.diaryMode passages`.
- AGENTS.md, README.md and DEVELOPMENT.md point at `Views/Diary/`.
