# Widget v2: two halves, and no Control Center control (21 Nov 2026)

Branch `widget-v2`, from `main` at 63a4ea8. Spec 9.8, "Changes from PJ, 21 Nov 2026".

## What PJ asked for
- Medium check-in widget: the stones were too small. Split it into two halves: "Noisy mind?" with the three buttons, and "Log stone?" with larger stones, each with its own "Noted at HH:MM".
- A haptic when a widget tap is noted.
- Control Center: first, a Dhamma Diary icon (the `waveform` looked like Voice Memos) and logging the level directly. After seeing it on the phone (the custom symbols didn't show), PJ asked to remove the control altogether.

## What changed
- **Medium widget** (`DhammaDiaryWidget/CheckInWidget.swift`): two halves with a divider. Left: "Noisy mind?" and Noisy / In between / Still stacked. Right: "Log stone?" with 52pt shaded stones (were 26pt). Each half has its own "Noted at …" line, which keeps its space so nothing shifts after a tap.
- **Separate noted times** (`WidgetLogQueue.NotedGroup`, `lastNoted(_:)`): a check-in tap and a stone tap are remembered separately. New test `testCheckInsAndStonesKeepTheirOwnNotedTime`.
- **Control removed:** `MindCheckInControl`, `OpenTodayIntent`, the `widget.openToday` flag and ContentView's `openTodayIfRequested()` are gone. A brief attempt at three level controls with custom symbols was dropped with it.

## Not possible
- Haptics from a widget: widget button actions run in the background and iOS gives widgets no haptic API.
