# Widget v2: two halves, direct-logging controls, custom symbols (21 Nov 2026)

Branch `widget-v2`, from `main` at 63a4ea8. Spec 9.8, "Changes from PJ, 21 Nov 2026".

## What PJ asked for
- Medium check-in widget: the stones were too small. Split it into two halves: "Noisy mind?" with the three buttons, and "Log stone?" with larger stones, each with its own "Noted at HH:MM".
- A haptic when a widget tap is noted.
- Control: the icon looked like Voice Memos (`waveform`); use the Dhamma Diary icon. And log the level directly, with a slider, instead of opening Today.

## What changed
- **Medium widget** (`DhammaDiaryWidget/CheckInWidget.swift`): two halves with a divider. Left: "Noisy mind?" and Noisy / In between / Still stacked. Right: "Log stone?" with 52pt shaded stones (were 26pt). Each half has its own "Noted at …" line, which keeps its space so nothing shifts after a tap.
- **Separate noted times** (`WidgetLogQueue.NotedGroup`, `lastNoted(_:)`): a check-in tap and a stone tap are remembered separately. New test `testCheckInsAndStonesKeepTheirOwnNotedTime`.
- **Three new controls** (iOS 18): "Mind: Still", "Mind: In between", "Mind: Noisy" run `LogMindCheckIntent` (10/50/90) without opening the app. "Mind check-in" still opens Today. Controls can only be buttons or toggles, so a slider isn't possible (PJ chose three controls).
- **Custom symbols** (`scripts/icon/make_control_symbols.py` → `Assets.xcassets/*.symbolset`): controls can only show a one-colour symbol, not the app icon. `stone.book` is a stone above an open book; the levels are a stone with a flat line (still), a few specks, or many specks, echoing the noise behind the stone on Today. Xcode requires a Regular-M glyph, so the script draws that size only.
- **Widget refresh**: both log intents call `WidgetCenter.shared.reloadTimelines(ofKind: "MindCheckIn")`, so a Control Center tap updates the widget's "Noted at …" line.

## Not possible
- Haptics from a widget: widget button actions run in the background and iOS gives widgets no haptic API.
