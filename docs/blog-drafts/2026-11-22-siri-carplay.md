# Hands on the wheel

Driving is when the mind gets noisy, but it's no time to look at a screen. Dhamma Diary now answers Siri: "Log a black stone in Dhamma Diary", "My mind is noisy in Dhamma Diary", or "What's tonight's question?", which Siri reads aloud. It works in CarPlay too. The small widgets are also trying out a simpler look for glancing at, in case CarPlay shows them: just the two stones, or just tonight's line.
