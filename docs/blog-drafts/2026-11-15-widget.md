# The question on your Lock Screen

The hardest moment for a quiet app is the moment you pick up your phone. Whatever is loudest wins.

Dhamma Diary now has a widget. It doesn't count anything or ask for anything. It shows tonight's question, on the Home Screen or under the clock on the Lock Screen: "Look back at today as if in a clear mirror…". Every time you reach for your phone, before anything else gets you, there's the question.

It changes at 3am, along with the rest of the app. Tap it and you're on tonight's page. It never shows your stones or what you wrote, because a Lock Screen is something other people see.
