## 1. What the app is

Dhamma Diary is a quiet daily journal for Dhamma practitioners on iPhone.

- **Each day:** one short passage from the suttas (Bhikkhu Sujato's English translation) and one reflection question, with an optional journal entry.
- **During the day:** optional white and black stones for wholesome and unwholesome thoughts, exactly as in White Stone.
- **Where data lives:** everything stays on the phone. There is no account, no analytics, no sync, no notifications except the mind check-ins the user turns on (section 9), and no network use at runtime, apart from the "Read the full sutta" links, which open Safari.

Out of scope for v1: iCloud sync, Android, importing White Stone data, browsing the canon live, merging with First Stone, any AI feature in the app.

## 3. Data

### 3.1 Reflection day (the 3am rule)
*From: the evening reflection flow; how passages, entries and the 3am day are stored.*

A **reflection day** runs from 03:00 to 03:00 the next morning, in the phone's current time zone. Before 03:00, it is still the previous day.

The rule applies **everywhere a date is grouped into a day**:
- the Diary tab: which passage is shown, which entry is edited, the stone summary
- Today's stones and balance
- Review's calendar, day detail, 14-day bars, all-time chart and stats line
- the pattern notes (`PatternEngine`)
- `Stone.dayKey`

Add to `DateHelpers`:
- `reflectionDay(for: Date) -> Date`: the calendar date (start of day) that `date` belongs to. If `date` is before 03:00 local on its own calendar date (`date(bySettingHour: 3, …)`), it's the previous date; otherwise its own. (Changed during the build, 2026-11-15: "subtract 3 hours" with `Calendar` arithmetic subtracts elapsed time, so 03:30 on a spring-forward morning lands on the previous day.)
- `reflectionDayInterval(for: Date) -> DateInterval`: from 03:00 on `reflectionDay(for:)` to 03:00 the next day, built with `Calendar`. Also `reflectionDayInterval(forDay:)` for a calendar date (a calendar cell, `JournalEntry.day`), `reflectionInterval(fromDay:days:)` and `reflectionMonthInterval(for:)`.
- `reflectionDayKey(for: Date) -> String`: `"yyyy-MM-dd"` of `reflectionDay(for:)`.

Then replace every `dayInterval(for:)`, `Calendar.current.startOfDay(for:)` and `dayKey(for:)` used for grouping with the reflection-day versions. That covers `TodayView`, `ReviewView`, `PatternEngine` (its private `dayKey`), `Stone.init`, `Stone.timestamp.didSet` and `DateHelpers.dayKeysForPastWeeks`. Keep `Stone.timestamp` and `dayKey` in sync: `dayKey = reflectionDayKey(for: timestamp)`. "Today" in UI copy means the current reflection day.

Tests (new):
- 02:59 belongs to the previous day; 03:00 belongs to the same day.
- A stone at 01:00 on the 15th has the 14th's `dayKey` and appears in the 14th's interval.
- A day that contains a daylight-saving change still has a correct interval. Use a fixed time zone with daylight saving in the test, such as `America/New_York`.

| `responseText` | `String` | what the user wrote |
| `createdAt` | `Date` | |
| `updatedAt` | `Date` | |

Rules, kept from White Stone's reflections:
- At most one entry per reflection day. Saving again overwrites it and updates `updatedAt`. The copies (`passageID`, ref, title, question) are not overwritten when editing a past entry.
- Saving empty or whitespace-only text deletes the entry.
- New entries can only be written for the **current** reflection day. A missed day can't be filled in afterwards. Past entries stay editable (from Review or Passages).

### 3.3 Passage set (bundled, read-only)
*From: how passages, entries and the 3am day are stored; PJ's passage set; the "no AI-derived use" decision.*

The passages ship as one generated JSON file in the app bundle: `DhammaDiary/Resources/passages.json`. It is never written to SwiftData. File order is sequence order.

```json
{
  "formatVersion": 1,
  "source": { "repo": "suttacentral/bilara-data", "branch": "published", "commit": "<sha>", "translator": "sujato" },
  "passages": [
    {
      "id": "an10.51-mirror",
      "ref": "AN 10.51",
      "title": "Your Own Mind",
      "uid": "an10.51",
      "ranges": [{ "start": "3.2", "end": "3.6" }],
      "blocks": [["Suppose there was a woman or man who was young, …", "…"]],
      "excerpt": ["Suppose there was a woman or man who was young, …"],
      "question": "Look back at today as if in a clear mirror: …",
      "url": "https://suttacentral.net/an10.51/en/sujato"
    }
  ]
}
```

- **`id`:** permanent and never reused, even if the passage is removed later. Rewording a question keeps the ID.
- **`ranges`:** one or more segment ranges from the same sutta. A single segment has `start == end`.
- **`blocks`:** one block per range, each a list of segment texts in order. The app shows each segment as its own line or paragraph, with a centred "…" between blocks.
- **`excerpt`:** a few segments from inside the passage, about 200 characters, for the passage widget (8.6). The source names it as one range (`"excerpt": {"start", "end"}`) that must lie inside one of the passage's ranges.
- **Text is Sujato's, verbatim.** The one exception is that the generator removes an unpaired `“ ” ‘ ’` only at the very first or very last character of an excerpt. No words are changed.

**Generator** (`scripts/passages/`, run by a developer, not by the app). It is a promoted version of `prototypes/passage-set/build.py` on branch `prototype/passage-set`.
- Input: `scripts/passages/passages-source.json` (id, ref, uid, ranges, question per passage, in order) and a local checkout of bilara-data's `published` branch (a sparse checkout of `translation/en/sujato/sutta` is enough).
- It reads `translation/en/sujato/sutta/**/<uid>_translation-en-sujato.json`. It takes the title from the last non-empty `:0.x` segment, then skips `0.x` header segments, empty segments and surrounding whitespace.
- It writes `passages.json` with the bilara-data commit SHA, and prints each passage's word count. Around 150 words is a guide, not a hard limit.
- It fails if an ID is duplicated, a range matches no segments, or a question is empty.

**App side:** a small `PassageSet` loader reads the file once (a plain `Codable` struct, no SwiftData). Tests check that it decodes, IDs are unique, every passage has text and a question, and the first passage is `an10.51-mirror`.

**v1 content:** the opening 13 below. More are added later by [Task] PJ completes the passage set after the opening 13; the app must work with whatever count ships.

### 3.4 Sequence (tonight's passage)

- On first launch, store the **first reflection day** once in `@AppStorage("sequence.firstDay")` as `"yyyy-MM-dd"`. It's never changed afterwards.
- Tonight's passage index = (whole calendar days from the first reflection day to the current reflection day) mod (passage count). Count days with `Calendar.dateComponents([.day], from:to:)` between the two dates' start-of-day, so it's DST-safe. If the result is negative (the clock was set back), use 0.
- It advances whether or not the app is opened; there's no backlog.
- Accepted side effect: when an update adds passages, someone on a later loop jumps position. Past entries are unaffected because they store their passage.

Tests: day 0 → passage 1; day 12 → passage 13; day 13 with 13 passages → passage 1; negative → passage 1.

## 4. Screens

### 4.1 Today
*Unchanged from White Stone* apart from: "today" means the current reflection day (so at 01:00, Today still shows the previous day's stones and balance), and the title text reads "Dhamma Diary". Stones are optional throughout the app; nothing anywhere treats a day without stones as missing.

### 4.2 Diary tab (replaces Reflections)
*From: the evening reflection flow; PJ's passage set (screen look).*

The tab is available at any time of day. There's no time-based switching and no reminders. It keeps White Stone's two-mode switch (`ReflectionMode`): **Tonight** (the default, was `today`) and **Passages** (history, was `byQuestion`).

**Tonight**, top to bottom:
1. **Stone summary** (read-only, quiet), only if the current reflection day has stones. For example "3 white · 1 black", with their roots. Nothing is shown on a day with no stones.
2. **Passage.**
   - The title, in the existing serif style.
   - A small reference line: "AN 10.51 · translated by Bhikkhu Sujato".
   - The excerpt in an **italic** serif at body size, with "…" between blocks.
   - A "Read the full sutta" link to `url`.
3. **Question**, in a **larger** serif than the passage. It's the visual anchor of the screen.
4. **Journal entry**: a free-text editor for the current reflection day, with a Save action. Keep White Stone's save behaviour and its quiet "Saved at …" note. Replace its helper text with: "Each day keeps one entry. Saving again replaces it."

**Passages** (history): past journal entries grouped by `passageID`, newest first. Each group shows the passage reference and title. Each entry shows its day, its question copy and its text. Opening an entry allows editing (empty deletes). This replaces White Stone's `ByQuestionView`; `ReflectionDetailView`'s "other responses to this question" becomes "other entries for this passage", matched by `passageID`.

**Three modes** (PJ, 25 Nov, replacing the two-mode switch): a segmented control under the title chooses **Tonight**, **Entries** or **Passages**.
- **Entries:** every journal entry by date, newest first. Each row shows only the date and the sutta ("SN 46.55 With Saṅgārava"); the entry text is hidden. Tapping the row drops it down to show that day's passage and question first (with the link to the full sutta), then the start of the entry, which opens the entry in full. (PJ, 26 Nov: the entry no longer shows straight away.)
- **Passages:** every passage in the app in sequence order, as "Night n · reference", title and question, with "Tonight" beside tonight's. Tapping one opens its text and the entries written for it. "All entries for this passage" (from Review) and Tonight's earlier-entries link open this mode at that passage.

### 4.3 Review
*From: what changes in Review, About and onboarding.*

- The stats line ("Total days tracked · This month · Streak"), the 14-day bars, the all-time chart and the pattern notes are unchanged and about stones only, including the streak count. They group by reflection day (3.1). The stats line sits at the top of Review, just under the title, followed by the Daily Review and This week links, then Mind today (PJ, 29 Nov 2026).
- In the Review tour, the button "Continue to Reflections" becomes "Continue to Diary".

## 8. Presence without noise (PJ, 15 Nov 2026)
*From: PJ's brainstorm on staying present beside noisier apps. PJ chose ideas 1, 2, 6 and 9. None of them adds a notification, badge, streak or count to keep up, so the rules in `AGENTS.md` are unchanged.*

The aim is for the app to be in view at the right moment and easy to start, without nudging.

### 8.1 Morning question on Today (idea 2)
- A quiet card at the top of Today, above the stone counts. It reads "Carry this through the day", then tonight's question in the Diary's serif, at most three lines. Tapping it opens Diary → Tonight.
- It shows all day, every reflection day, whether or not an entry has been written. If no passage loads, it doesn't appear.

### 8.2 Starting from the day's stones (idea 6)
- On Tonight, between the question and the journal box, a small line: "You had 2 white stones and 1 black stone today. What was happening?"
- A colour with no stones is left out ("You had 2 white stones today. What was happening?"). Singular and plural follow the count. With no stones at all, the line doesn't appear.
- It's display only. Nothing is inserted into the entry.

### 8.3 The week's look-back (idea 9)
- **Week:** Monday to Sunday, in reflection days (3.1), containing the current reflection day.
- **Where:** on Sundays, Tonight shows a "Look back on this week" link below Save. Review has a "This week" link under the stats line, so the page can be reached any day.
- **The page**, titled "This week", with the date range (e.g. "9–15 November"):
  - the week's stones: "9 white · 4 black", and the roots with counts, most frequent first;
  - one row per day from Monday up to the current day: the day, its white and black stone counts if any, and, if there's an entry, the passage reference and title and the entry's first line. Tapping an entry opens it.
  - **Mind line per day** (PJ, 22 Nov): on the right of each row, the stone counts with the day's mind chart under them, in Today's style but 128 × 56pt with three hour labels (8am, 2pm, 8pm). A day without check-ins shows no chart. The passage reference stays on one line.
- A day with nothing logged is just its name; nothing reads as missing. No totals compare weeks.

### 8.4 Widget (idea 1)
- A WidgetKit extension, `DhammaDiaryWidget` (bundle ID `com.whitestone.DhammaDiary.Widget`), with two widgets in one kind:
  - **Home Screen, medium:** "TONIGHT · AN 10.51" above tonight's question in serif.
  - **Lock Screen, rectangular:** the question alone, truncated to fit.
- Tapping either opens Diary → Tonight (URL `dhammadiary://tonight`).
- The widget works out tonight's passage itself from the bundled `passages.json` and the stored first day (3.4). The first day moves to a shared App Group, `group.com.whitestone.DhammaDiary`. On launch the app copies an existing value across once, so the sequence doesn't restart.
- The timeline changes at the next 03:00. The app also asks for a widget refresh when it becomes active.
- It shows no stones and no journal text: the Lock Screen is visible to others.

### 8.6 Passage widget (PJ, 22 Nov 2026)
- A second Home Screen widget, "Tonight's passage" (kind `TonightPassage`, `DhammaDiaryWidget/PassageWidget.swift`). It sits beside the question widget, which is unchanged.
  - **Medium:** "TONIGHT · AN 4.14" above the passage's `excerpt` in italic serif, up to five lines. The longest excerpt (AN 10.61) ends in "…".
  - **Large:** the excerpt, then a divider and tonight's question at the bottom.
- Same timeline (changes at 03:00), the same tap target (`dhammadiary://tonight`), and no stones or journal text.
- No Lock Screen size: a rectangular Lock Screen widget is too small for an excerpt.

### 8.7 Sutta links and excerpts (PJ, 22 Nov 2026)
- Wherever an entry shows its passage ("AN 10.61 · Ignorance"), in "This week" and on a journal entry, the line is an accent link with ↗ that opens the sutta on SuttaCentral. An entry whose passage is no longer bundled shows the line as plain grey text.
- A journal entry shows its passage's excerpt (3.3) in grey italic serif, between the reference and the question.
- "This week" rows open their entry with a tap anywhere outside the link. They aren't NavigationLinks, which would swallow taps on the link.
- Tonight leaves 32pt below "Look back on this week", so it clears the floating tab bar.
- On Sundays (reflection day), Review opens with a "Look back on this week" card above Mind today. On those days the small "This week" link under the stats is hidden; on other days it stays.

### 8.8 Always dark (PJ, 22 Nov 2026)
- The app is always in dark mode, whatever the phone is set to: light mode is too stimulating. Set with `UIUserInterfaceStyle: Dark` in `project.yml`, which covers every screen, sheet and alert, and the launch screen.
- The widgets still follow the phone's appearance.

### 8.9 Lock Screen widgets and the passage's line (PJ, 22 Nov 2026)
- **Highlight:** each passage has a one-line `highlight`, its essence, under about 100 characters. It is a verbatim piece of one segment. The source gives it as text, and the generator checks that it appears in the passage. The app adds "…" where the sentence carries on before or after (`Passage.highlightText`).

## 10. Daily Review (PJ, 28 Nov 2026)
Trello: "Dhamma Diary: Daily Review (stones dropped into your hand, then the day so far)". A quiet look at the day so far: the current reflection day (3am boundary).

### 10.1 Where
- Review gets a **"Daily Review ›"** link just above **"This week ›"**, under the stats line at the top of Review. On Sundays, when "This week" gives way to the card at the top, Daily Review stays there on its own. (Both moved to the top with the stats line, 29 Nov 2026.)
- It opens a screen titled **"Daily Review"**.

### 10.2 The stones fall
- The day's stones fall one by one, in the order they were logged, into **two heaps**: white from the top-left corner into the left heap, black from the top-right corner into the right heap. An invisible full-height wall between them (with a small gap) keeps the heaps apart. (PJ, 28 Nov: one mixed heap of 40 was a mess.) Each heap sits in a shallow hollow, like stones dropped into a cupped hand.
- **A composite stone** (PJ, 28 Nov): once a stone lands it leaves gravity and is drawn towards its heap's centre, harder up and down than sideways, so each heap packs into the oval outline of one large stone made of the small ones. The oval is sized to the count (half as wide again as tall). Later stones fall in and join it. Physics with SpriteKit (`SpriteView`): gravity, a small bounce, stones knocking against each other and settling into a heap. Stones are drawn like Today's stone, slightly oval.
- Each landing has a sound and a Core Haptics tap, scaled by how hard it lands:
  - the first stone onto each empty palm: a soft, fleshy tap;
  - a stone landing on the pile: heavier and duller;
  - stones touching afterwards: a light clack.
- **Tuned on the phone** (PJ, 28 Nov: no sound could be heard, and the haptics were too weak): the sounds' character sits between 400 Hz and 4 kHz, which a phone speaker can play (the first version was mostly below 200 Hz). Even a gentle landing plays at 60% or more. A landing is felt as a full knock followed by a brief, heavy settle; a clack is a small crisp tick.
- Sounds are short synthesised samples made by `scripts/sounds/make_stone_sounds.py` (no third-party audio, so no licence to track). They play in the `.ambient` audio session: the silent switch mutes them, and the user's music keeps playing.
- Timing: the interval between drops shortens as the count grows, so the fall takes about 4 seconds at most (0.55s apart for a few stones, never under 0.05s). Stones are smaller on a big day so each heap stays in its half..
- **Tap to skip:** a tap during the fall drops the rest at once, without sound, and shows the summary.
- **Zero stones:** no animation; the summary shows straight away.
- **Reduce Motion:** no falling; the stones are placed in their ovals, gather out of sight and fade in, without sound.
- The pile stays still after it settles; tapping it doesn't drop them again.

### 10.3 Then the day so far
After the stones settle, fading in:
- **Stones:** "n white · n black", or "No stones yet today."
- **Mind:** the day's mind chart (as in Review, not zoomable) and its summary line, or "No mind check-ins yet today."
- **Diary:** today's entry with its passage reference and title, opening the entry; or "No diary entry yet."
- Tone (AGENTS.md): no celebration, no counting up, no judgement of white against black.

### 10.4 This week opens the same way (PJ, 28 Nov 2026)
- **This week** (spec 8.3, from Review's link, the Sunday card and Tonight's Sunday link) begins like Daily Review: the week's stones, Monday to today in logging order, fall into a white and a black heap that gather into composite stones. Then the existing This week review (the range, "n white · n black", roots, and each day with its stones, mind chart and entry) fades in below.
- Same sounds, haptics, tap to skip, Reduce Motion and zero-stone rules as 10.2. A week can hold many more stones, so they shrink as needed to keep each composite stone within its half and the height of the space, and landing sounds are spaced at least 0.04s apart.
- Coming back from an entry keeps the settled heaps; it doesn't fall again.
