A quiet place to end the day with the Buddha's words.

Each day, Dhamma Diary offers one short passage from the early Buddhist discourses and a single question to reflect on. Write as much or as little as you like, or simply sit with it. Tomorrow brings the next passage.

During the day, you can log white and black stones: a white stone for a wholesome thought or action, a black stone for an unwholesome one. Press and slide on the stone to note how noisy or still your mind is right now, and see the shape of the day as a simple line.

• One passage and one question each day, in a fixed sequence that begins with the Buddha's mirror simile
• An optional journal entry for each day, kept by day and by passage, with a link to the full sutta
• Optional white and black stones, with a calendar and gentle trends in Review
• Mind check-ins, with optional reminders a few times a day
• A look back on the week every Sunday
• Widgets for the Home Screen and Lock Screen: tonight's question, a line from the passage, and quick buttons to log a stone or note your mind
• Siri: "Log a white stone in Dhamma Diary"
• A day that ends at 3am, so late-night reflections count for the day you lived
• Always in a calm dark theme
• Everything stays on your phone: no account, no tracking, no ads

Passages are from Bhikkhu Sujato's translations, published by SuttaCentral and dedicated to the public domain.
