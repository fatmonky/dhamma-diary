# Glossary

Terms used in Dhamma Diary (the app's name on the App Store and home screen). Planning decisions live on the Trello map "MAP: Dhamma Diary on a White Stone fork" (https://trello.com/c/UKufqlKx).

**Stone**: one logged moment from the day, either white (wholesome) or black (unwholesome). Optional: a day can have none.

**Root**: the kind of thought a stone came from. White stones: renunciation, kindness, harmlessness. Black stones: sensual desire, ill will, harming. A stone's root must match its colour.

**Passage**: a short excerpt from one sutta, shown in the evening with one reflection question. An excerpt may join several parts of the same sutta, marked with an ellipsis. Passages come from a fixed, ordered set that ships with the app.

**Evening reflection**: the nightly passage, its question, and the user's optional journal entry about the day.

**Reflection day**: the day an evening reflection, a stone or anything else in the app belongs to. It runs until 3am, so a reflection written or a stone logged just after midnight counts for the day just lived.

**Passage set**: the ordered list of passages that ships with the app. Each passage keeps its identity for good, even if later versions reword its question or drop it from the set.

**Sequence**: each user's walk through the passage set. It starts at passage 1 on the user's first reflection day and moves on one passage per reflection day, whether or not the app is opened, looping back to passage 1 after the last.

**Journal entry**: what the user writes in an evening reflection. Optional, at most one per reflection day, and editable later. It remembers the passage and question it answered, as they were that day. A missed day can't be written afterwards.

**Diary**: the tab where the evening reflection lives, next to Today, Review and About. It can be opened at any time of day.

**Passages**: the history inside Diary, where past journal entries are gathered under the passage they answered. Review also shows each day's entry alongside its stones.
