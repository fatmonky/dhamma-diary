2. PURPOSE AND AUDIENCE
Dhamma Diary is a daily reflection journal for people who practise Buddhist meditation and study the early Buddhist discourses (suttas).
Each day it shows one short sutta passage and one reflection question, with space to write a private journal entry.
During the day, the user can log "stones": a white stone for a wholesome thought or action, a black stone for an unwholesome one. They can also note how noisy or still their mind is.
Review shows the day and the week so far.
The value is a quiet, private habit of daily reflection grounded in the Buddha's words. It has no accounts, ads, tracking or gamification.

3. HOW TO ACCESS THE MAIN FEATURES
No login, credentials, sample files or network connection are needed. All content is bundled with the app.
- On first launch, a short onboarding walks through Today, logging a first stone, Review and Diary. At the end, it asks whether to turn on mind check-in reminders. These are optional, and choosing them shows the iOS notification permission prompt.
- Today: swipe the stone left or right to choose white or black, then hold to log it. Press and slide the stone up or down to note how noisy or still the mind is.
- Review: a calendar of stones, stats, the mind chart, and the "Daily Review" and "This week" links. These play a short animation of the stones falling, with sound and haptics, then show the summary.
- Diary: Tonight shows the day's passage and question. Type an entry and tap Save. "Read the full sutta" opens suttacentral.net in Safari. Entries and Passages list past entries and the whole passage set.
- Widgets (Home Screen and Lock Screen) and Siri phrases such as "Log a white stone in Dhamma Diary" are optional extras.
- The passage changes once a day, at 3am. On a fresh install, the reviewer sees passage 1.

4. EXTERNAL SERVICES
None. The app makes no network requests and includes no third-party SDKs: no analytics, crash reporting, authentication, payment processing, AI services, cloud sync or backend.
All data is stored on the device with SwiftData.
It uses only Apple frameworks (SwiftUI, SwiftData, WidgetKit, App Intents, UserNotifications, SpriteKit, Core Haptics, AVFoundation).
Reminders are local notifications scheduled on the device.
The only things that leave the app are user-tapped links: the full sutta on suttacentral.net, a public-domain source on sacred-texts.com, and a feedback email from About.

5. REGIONAL DIFFERENCES
None. The app's content and features are the same in every region, with no region or locale checks. The app is in English. Dates and times follow the device's settings.
